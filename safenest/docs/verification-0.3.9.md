The existing two third-party feeds contain 6,673 distinct gambling hostnames. Seven researched Bangladesh-related hostnames are now bundled separately, bringing the imported total to 6,680. The small starter and signed catalog may add other rules. The cited observations are historical; sites and ownership can change. `data/bangladesh-gambling-research.json` records evidence and review-only leads.

Two exact APK package IDs are included in the optional Accessibility app guard. Public app descriptions claim sports betting, but neither APK was downloaded or independently reverse engineered. Matching only a label such as “Babu88” risks blocking unrelated puzzle apps; users can select additional installed apps in SafeNest. On a personal phone the user can revoke Accessibility or install another VPN. Device Owner policies are separate and require eligible managed enrollment.
