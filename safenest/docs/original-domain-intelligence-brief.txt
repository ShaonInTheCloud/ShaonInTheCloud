4. CONFIDENCE SYSTEM
==================================================

Every domain must have one of these states:

CONFIRMED
HIGH
SUSPECTED
MIRROR
DEAD
FALSE_POSITIVE
REVIEW_REQUIRED

CONFIRMED:
Strong evidence that the domain operates gambling/betting/casino services.

HIGH:
Multiple reliable indicators strongly suggest gambling.

SUSPECTED:
Some indicators exist but evidence is insufficient.

MIRROR:
Known or strongly verified mirror/alternate domain belonging to an identified gambling service.

DEAD:
Previously relevant gambling domain that no longer resolves/operates.

FALSE_POSITIVE:
Investigated and determined not to be gambling.

REVIEW_REQUIRED:
Automated detection found it but human verification is needed.

Only sufficiently high-confidence records should enter the active blocking list automatically.
