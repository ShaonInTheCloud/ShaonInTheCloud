Payment status

How you will pay

Two methods are being prepared for the Global edition. Both open on the provider’s own secure page; SafeNest never stores card numbers or wallet keys.

Card — Mastercard or Visa

Processed by Stripe Checkout in US dollars. Your bank converts the charge to your currency and may add a small foreign-transaction fee.

Cryptocurrency

USDT, BTC, ETH and other coins through a hosted crypto checkout. The price is fixed in US dollars when you open the payment page. Network fees are shown before you send, and the payment is final once confirmed on-chain.

Before subscriptions open

Final prices, taxes, device limits, cancellation and refund terms will be published before checkout opens. There is no automatic renewal: a plan ends when its paid period ends.

Does creating an account cost anything?

No. Creating an account does not purchase a subscription or start device protection.

Are these prices final?

No. All three prices are planned offers. The final offer and terms will appear in your account before any payment becomes available.

Can I get a refund on a crypto payment?

Card refunds follow the published refund terms. Crypto payments cannot be reversed on-chain, so a refund, where the terms allow one, is sent manually to a wallet address you confirm.

I live in Bangladesh — which edition should I use?

The Bangladesh edition, priced in taka. The protection features are the same.

Try SafeNest for three days

One 72-hour trial per confirmed account. The trial starts when you choose to begin it, and protection ends when the verified period expires. A compatible Android update must pass testing before trials open to customers.
