What SafeNest is designed to do

Gambling sites

The DNS filter checks requested domains against a signed, locally stored blocklist of betting and casino domains before the request leaves your phone. Matching happens on the device.

Adult sites

Listed adult-content domains can be blocked before a DNS request is forwarded. You decide whether this category is on.

Selected apps

With your permission, the app guard returns you to the home screen when a selected blocked app or a detected VPN app opens, so a filter you set cannot be quietly switched off.

How you can pay

Global checkout is being prepared with two methods. Both are hosted by the payment provider: SafeNest never sees your card number or wallet keys.

Card

Mastercard and Visa through Stripe Checkout, charged in US dollars. Your bank may add a small foreign-transaction fee.

Cryptocurrency

USDT, BTC, ETH and other coins through a hosted crypto checkout. The amount is fixed in US dollars at the moment you pay; on-chain confirmation can take a few minutes and the payment is final once confirmed.

There is no automatic renewal. A plan ends when its paid period ends, and you decide whether to pay again.

Questions people ask first

Does this website block anything?

No. Blocking runs in the Android app. The website lets you manage an account, read setup steps and, when checkout opens, pay for a plan.

Will it block every site or VPN?

No blocker is perfect, and SafeNest will never claim 100% blocking. The DNS filter only sees domains it is allowed to see, encrypted DNS inside some browsers can bypass it, and a determined person can remove the app. SafeNest is a help for people who want to stay away, not a locked door. Read the setup and limitations.

Is my browsing history uploaded?

No. Domain matching happens on your phone against a locally stored blocklist. The server only knows whether your account has an active paid period.

Can I pay in my own currency?

Global plans are priced in US dollars. Your card issuer converts the charge; crypto payments are quoted in USD and settled in the coin you choose.
