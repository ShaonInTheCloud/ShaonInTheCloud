What the game is pretending to do

Every pop-up in Nest Run is a copy of the ones that reach phones in Bangladesh and everywhere else: a bonus that must be claimed in sixty seconds, a jackpot banner, a casino that looks like a sports site. The rabbit has to jump, slide and keep going. That is tiring, which is the point.

What the app actually does

SafeNest for Android keeps a signed list of gambling and adult-content domains on your phone and answers their DNS requests with nothing. Sites never load, so there is nothing to dodge. Matching happens on the device; your browsing history is not uploaded. It is a help for people who want to stay away, not a locked door, and it will never claim to block everything.
