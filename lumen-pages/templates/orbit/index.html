Three parts. No screws, no bulb.

Halo slots together in under a minute and comes apart the same way, so a scratched ring or a tired driver is a swap, not a replacement lamp.

  • Ring

    28 cm, 2700 to 4000 K, 1,100 lumensThe LEDs sit behind a diffuser on the inner edge, so you see the glow, never the diodes. Colour temperature follows the dimmer: lower is warmer.

  • Stem

    Anodised aluminium, 38 cmCarries power through a sprung contact in the collar. Lift the ring off to clean it, drop it back on, done.

  • Base

    Cast zinc, 1.6 kg, one dialHeavy enough that a cat cannot tip it. The dial is the only control: turn for brightness, press to switch off, press and hold to save a level.

  • Power

    USB-C, 30 W, 2 m braided cableRuns from the charger you already own. Driver is in the base, where it stays cool and can be swapped in warranty.
