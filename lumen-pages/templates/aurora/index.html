What “on-device” actually means

Unplug the Wi-Fi mid-meeting. Nothing changes.

The transcript, the summary and the search index live in ~/Library/Application Support/Northlight, encrypted with a key held in the Secure Enclave. Delete the folder and the meeting is gone. There is no copy anywhere else, because we never had one.
