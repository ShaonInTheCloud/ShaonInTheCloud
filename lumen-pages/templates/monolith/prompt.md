# Monolith — Portfolio
A dark, warm-charcoal portfolio for Jonas Weir, an invented 3D artist in Rotterdam who renders dense physical objects (furniture, audio, stone, tools) as stills and loops for launches, catalogues and exhibitions. The hero is one tall slab built in CSS 3D that leans toward the pointer while a specular highlight slides across its face, as if a studio key light were tracking you. Everything else is heavy wide type at extreme sizes and plain ruled lists: six works, four weeks, three rates, one email. It suits CGI artists, photographers, sculptors and industrial designers who want the page to feel like an object rather than a feed.

## Tokens
- Base `#1a1614` (charcoal with a brown undertone), base-2 `#201b18` for bands, surface `#2a2420` for the lead rate cell and previews
- Line `rgba(255,226,196,.13)`
- Text `#f1e9e0` (15:1), secondary text `#b5a99c` (7.5:1)
- Brass accent `#e0b27c` with ink `#241a10` for the filled button (9:1)
- Slab faces: front `#2b2521`, sides `#1e1917` (right side has a vertical gradient to `#171311`), top `#3a322c`, bottom `#141110`
- Specular light as an RGB triplet `--spec: 255,232,200` used by every glow
- Slab dimensions on `:root`: `--w clamp(150px, 22vw, 250px)`, `--h clamp(300px, 44vw, 500px)`, `--d clamp(54px, 8vw, 92px)`; smaller under 860 px
- One font family: Archivo, variable, with its width axis (wdth 62–125) and weight 300–900. The whole page is set in it. The hero and final headline use `"wdth" 125` at weight 800 (wide, tight, `letter-spacing -.035em`) so the type reads as a slab itself; work titles use `"wdth" 112` at 700; body stays at width 100, weight 400–600; rate figures use weight 300 at width 118. One family with a width axis gives the contrast of two faces without a second request.
- Max width 1200 px, gutter `clamp(16px, 4.5vw, 56px)`, 2 px radius buttons, no shadows except the slab's floor glow.

## Motion
- **The monolith (the one memorable moment).** `.mono` is `transform-style: preserve-3d` with `transform: rotateX(var(--rx)) rotateY(var(--ry))`. Six `.f` faces are absolutely centred (`translate: -50% -50%`) and pushed out: front/back `translateZ(±d/2)`, left/right `rotateY(∓90deg) translateZ(w/2)` at width `d`, top/bottom `rotateX(±90deg) translateZ(h/2)` at height `d`. `backface-visibility: hidden` on all. The front face has a `::before` with a faint diagonal sheen and 1 px horizontal grain, and a `::after` (inset −40%, `mix-blend-mode: screen`) holding the specular: `radial-gradient(42% 64% at var(--hx) var(--hy), rgba(spec,.6), rgba(spec,.14) 30%, transparent 70%)`. The left face's `::after` is a soft edge light whose opacity is `--edge`. The `.floor` under the slab is a blurred radial glow whose centre also follows `--hx`.
- `--rx`, `--ry` and `--hx` are registered with `@property` so they can be animated. While nobody has touched the hero, `.mono:not(.live)` runs `idle`: 14 s ease-in-out alternate from `rotateX(-4deg) rotateY(12deg) --hx 26%` to `rotateX(2deg) rotateY(34deg) --hx 48%`, so the slab slowly turns and the light slides across it.
- On the first mouse `pointermove` over the hero, the script reads the computed `--rx/--ry/--hx` (the current animated values) into its smoothed state, adds `.live` (which stops the keyframes) and from then on lerps toward targets each frame: yaw `22 + px*40` deg, pitch `-4 - py*18` deg, highlight `hx = 50 - px*90 %`, `hy = 40 - py*70 %`, where `px, py` are the pointer offset from the hero centre in −.5…+.5. Rotation lerps at .08 per frame, the highlight at .1, so the light leads the tilt slightly. `--edge` is `0.3 + yaw/60` so the left face brightens as it turns into view. The loop stops itself when every value is within .05 of its target and restarts on the next event. `pointerleave`, or 2.6 s without movement, eases back to the resting pose (22°, −4°, 34%, 28%). On phones, if `deviceorientation` is available without a permission prompt, gamma/beta drive a gentle version of the same targets; otherwise the slab simply idles.
- Hero h1, lede and CTA rise 16 px and fade in over .9 s with `cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)`, staggered 0 / .12 / .22 s.
- Work rows: on hover or focus the row text brightens, the row shifts 0.6 rem right over .3 s, and the preview fades in over .3 s while scaling from .96 to 1.
- Nothing else animates. No scroll-triggered reveals.
- **Reduced motion:** the idle keyframes and the hero rise live inside `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`; the script returns immediately when `(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` matches, so the slab stays in its resting pose with the highlight fixed at 34% / 28%, fully rendered. Smooth scrolling is turned off. Hover previews still appear (they are user-initiated), but the transition can be shortened to 0 if wanted.

## Rebuild instructions
1. Create one page and link Archivo from Google Fonts with the width axis: `family=Archivo:ital,wdth,wght@0,62..125,300..900;1,62..125,300..900`. Put all colours, the `--spec` triplet, the slab dimensions and the font on `:root`; register `--rx` (angle, −4deg), `--ry` (angle, 22deg) and `--hx` (percentage, 34%, `inherits: true`) with `@property`.
2. Build the sticky `<header>` with `<nav aria-label="Primary">`: brand link (name + muted discipline span), a `<ul>` of three links, outlined `.btn`. Hide the list and the discipline span under 760 px.
3. Hero: a relative, overflow-hidden `<section class="hero">` with a `.hero-bg` radial wash and a `.wrap` grid (1.15fr / .85fr). Left column: `<h1>` with a `<b>` for the light-weight phrase, `.lede`, and `.cta` (filled link + `<small>`). Right column: `<div class="stage" id="stage">` containing `<div class="mono" id="mono" aria-hidden="true">` with six `<div class="f front|back|left|right|top|bottom">` and a sibling `<div class="floor" id="floor">`.
4. Style the faces as described in Motion (centred with `translate`, then `transform` per face). Give `.stage` `display: grid; place-items: center; perspective: 1500px; perspective-origin: 50% 40%; touch-action: pan-y` and a height of `calc(var(--h) + 120px)`.
5. Write the pointer script (about 50 lines): smoothed `yaw, pitch, hx, hy` and targets; a `frame()` that lerps, writes `--ry`, `--rx`, `--hx`, `--hy`, `--edge` on `.mono` and `--hx` on `.floor`, and re-requests itself only while not settled; a `takeover()` that reads `getComputedStyle(mono).getPropertyValue('--ry' | '--rx' | '--hx')` into the smoothed values before adding `.live`; `pointermove` (mouse only) on the hero computing `px, py` and setting targets plus a 2.6 s idle timer; `pointerleave` and the timer calling `rest()`; an optional `deviceorientation` listener guarded by `!('requestPermission' in DeviceOrientationEvent)`. Return early when reduced motion is preferred.
6. Work list: `<section>` with the head and an `<ol class="list">`. Each `<li>` is `position: relative` and holds an `<a>` grid row (h3, client span, `<time>`, kind span) followed by `<div class="pv pv-sphere" aria-hidden="true">` etc. Style `.pv` as absolute, right 4%, vertically centred, `aspect-ratio: 4/3`, surface background with a hairline border, `opacity: 0; scale: .96; pointer-events: none`, shown by `li:hover .pv, li:focus-within .pv`. Draw each preview with `::before`/`::after` gradients only. Hide `.pv` under 760 px and reflow the row grid to `1fr auto` with the kind span spanning both columns.
7. Process: a base-2 `<section>` with the `1fr 1.3fr` grid, an h2 and intro on the left, and `<ol class="stages">` with `7rem 1fr` rows, brass `<time>`, h3 and paragraph. Collapse to one column under 860 px and single-column rows under 480 px.
8. Rates: a two-column head and a `.rate-grid` (three `<article class="rate">` with a right hairline, the middle one `.lead` on surface). Figures at `clamp(2.2rem, 4vw, 3.4rem)`, weight 300, `"wdth" 118`, with a block `<small>`. Stack under 860 px with bottom hairlines instead.
9. Clients: a `<section aria-label="Clients">` whose `.wrap` is a flex row with a top hairline, a `<strong>` label and a `<ul>` of names.
10. Final CTA: a relative `<section id="book">` with a `::before` radial glow at the top centre, a `1.3fr 1fr` grid, h2 at width 125, paragraph, and two buttons. Then the `<footer>` row.
11. Add `:focus-visible { outline: 2px solid var(--brass); outline-offset: 4px }`, `html { scroll-behavior: smooth }` with a reduce override, and keep the `idle` and `rise` keyframes inside the no-preference media query.
12. Test at 375 px: the slab renders above the headline at the reduced dimensions without clipping, the headline fits in two to four lines, rows show title/client and year/kind, the rates stack, and there is no horizontal scroll. Confirm one `<h1>` and header/nav/main/footer landmarks; the slab is `aria-hidden` because it is decorative.
