Ten weeks, four stages, one room

An identity project runs on a fixed calendar. You know what you get at the end of each stage before it starts, and nobody presents more than three directions.

  1. Weeks 1–2

    Listening

    We visit the factory, the shop, the warehouse. We talk to whoever answers the phone. We read the old brand guidelines you stopped using.

  2. Weeks 3–5

    Three directions

    Three routes, each taken far enough to be printed, worn and put on a shelf. You choose one. We do not blend them.

  3. Weeks 6–9

    Building it out

    Type, colour, packaging dielines, signage drawings, templates for the people who will actually use them.

  4. Week 10

    Handover and press

    Files, a short plain-language manual and a day at the printer or sign maker with you. We stay reachable afterwards.
