# Atelier — Agency / Studio
A warm plaster page for Pell & Marrow, an invented four-person design studio in Porto that makes identities, packaging and wayfinding for companies that manufacture physical things. The mood is a sunlit studio wall: linen and plaster tones, one oversized high-contrast serif headline that a slow diagonal light sweeps across letter by letter, and below it a row of case studies you can drag like prints on a table. Everything after the hero is ruled lists, sentence-case copy and real numbers (ten weeks, €6,800, two openings in January). It suits studios, architects, small agencies and craft brands who want to feel established rather than loud.

## Tokens
- Plaster (page) `#efe6d9`, linen (bands, plates) `#e6dac9`, paper (hover, light text on ink) `#f6efe4`
- Ink `#241f1a`, secondary ink `#5c5248` (7.2:1 on plaster), line `rgba(60,45,30,.16)`
- Rust accent `#a9472f` with `#fbf3ea` text for the filled button (5.6:1)
- Sweep light: `--glow #b56d28`, `--glow-2 #d9a35f` (only ever shown as a transient highlight inside glyphs)
- A fixed `body::before` adds two faint radial washes (warm white top-left, tan bottom-right) at 55% opacity so the plaster is not flat.
- Display: Bodoni Moda, variable (opsz 6–96, weight 400–900, italic). Chosen for its extreme thick/thin contrast: at 11 rem the hairlines turn the sweeping light into something that looks engraved, and the italic "shape." carries the rust accent without a second colour block. Optical size is set per use (96 for the hero, 72 for h2, 24–36 for small headings) so the hairlines stay printable at small sizes.
- Body: Hanken Grotesk 400–600. A quiet grotesque with a tall x-height that sits comfortably next to a Didone without competing; it does the lists, prices and buttons.
- Max width 1180 px, gutter `clamp(16px, 4.5vw, 56px)`, 2 px radius on buttons, no shadows anywhere.

## Layout
1. **Nav** — sticky, 68 px, translucent plaster with blur and a hairline bottom border. Brand "Pell & Marrow" in Bodoni with an italic rust ampersand, four links centred (hidden under 760 px), outlined "Start a project" button right.
2. **Hero** — top padding ~9vh. The `<h1>` is `clamp(3.5rem, 12.4vw, 11.6rem)`, line-height .92, three lines on desktop ("Design that / holds its / shape."), with "shape." in italic rust. Under it a two-column row (1.1fr / .9fr, aligned to the bottom): a 30-ch lede in secondary ink on the left, the filled rust CTA plus an 18-ch availability note on the right. Single column under 760 px.
3. **Work strip** — hairline top rule. Head row: h2 "Six things we have put into the world" with a one-line instruction, and two round outlined arrow buttons on the right (hidden on phones). The strip is a flex row with `overflow-x: auto`, `scroll-snap-type: x mandatory`, hidden scrollbar, that bleeds into the right gutter. Cards alternate widths (600 px "wide" and 420 px; 86vw / 78vw on phones). Each card is an `<a>` with a bordered linen "plate" drawn entirely in CSS (concentric rings and a rust arch; vertical planks with rust rules; a dot grain fading out; a single diagonal with a rust circle; shelf rules with a dark book; a grid with a rust block), then an h3 and a `<dl>` of client, scope, year with visually-hidden `<dt>`s. Hover lightens the plate to paper and scales the pattern 3%.
4. **Process** — linen band. Head: h2 "Ten weeks, four stages, one room" beside a 42-ch paragraph. Then an `<ol>` of four columns with a solid ink top rule and hairline dividers: italic rust `<time>` (Weeks 1–2, 3–5, 6–9, Week 10), h3, 30-ch paragraph. 2-up under 900 px, 1-up under 520 px.
5. **Studio** — two columns: a Bodoni blockquote from a client (with a plain attribution) left; a `<dl>` of five studio facts right as ruled two-column rows (Founded, People, Projects a year, Languages, Not for us).
6. **Formats** — h2 "Three ways to work with us", then three `<article>` rows with solid ink rules: name + 34-ch description / three-item plain list / Bodoni price with a small qualifier / outlined button. 2-column under 860 px, 1-column under 520 px.
7. **Final CTA** — full-width ink section. h2 "Tell us what you make." with "make." italic in glow-2, a 36-ch paragraph, an outlined paper button and the email and phone in small text, right-aligned.
8. **Footer** — one flex row: copyright left, four links right.

## Motion
- **Light-swept headline (the one memorable moment).** A tiny script splits each word of the `<h1>` into `<span class="ch">` letters, each carrying `--i` (its index) and the h1 carrying `--n` (total). Each letter gets a 112° linear gradient (ink 0–38%, glow-2 46%, glow 50%, glow-2 54%, ink 62–100%) sized 260% wide, clipped to the glyph with `background-clip: text` and `color: transparent`. The keyframe moves `background-position` from 100% to 0% over 7.5 s linear, so the highlight travels diagonally through the glyph; `animation-delay: calc((var(--n) - var(--i)) * -.13s)` phases the letters so the band crosses the whole headline left to right in about 3 s, then the headline rests in plain ink for about 4 s before the next pass. Letters inside the italic `<em>` use the same gradient with rust instead of ink. Nothing is lit on hover; the sweep is ambient.
- Hero lede row rises 14 px and fades in over .9 s, `cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)`, delayed .25 s.
- **Draggable strip.** On `pointerdown` with a mouse, the strip records `scrollLeft`, adds `.drag` (which sets `scroll-snap-type: none`, `cursor: grabbing`, `user-select: none`) and captures the pointer; `pointermove` sets `scrollLeft = start - dx`; `pointerup`/`pointercancel` removes `.drag` after 120 ms so snapping re-engages on the nearest card. A capture-phase click handler swallows the click if the pointer moved more than 4 px, so a drag never opens a case. Touch is left to native scrolling and snapping. The arrow buttons `scrollBy` one card width with smooth behaviour; the strip is focusable and ArrowLeft/ArrowRight scroll it.
- Plate patterns scale 1.03 on hover over .5 s. Nothing else moves; no scroll-triggered reveals.
- **Reduced motion:** the gradient, clip and keyframes live inside `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`, so with reduce the letters are plain ink and rust, fully visible from first paint. The hero rise and plate scale are off. `scroll-behavior: smooth` should also be disabled under reduce. Dragging still works because it is user-initiated, but snapping re-engages instantly.

## Rebuild instructions
1. Create one page with Bodoni Moda (ital, opsz 6..96, wght 400..900) and Hanken Grotesk (400..600) from Google Fonts. Put every colour above on `:root` plus `--display`, `--body`, `--max`, `--gutter`. Set `body` to plaster with `overflow-x: hidden` and add the fixed `::before` with the two radial washes.
2. Build the sticky `<header>` with `<nav aria-label="Primary">`: brand link, a `<ul>` of four links, and an outlined `.btn`. Hide the list under 760 px.
3. In `<main>`, write the hero `<h1 id="lux" aria-label="Design that holds its shape.">` with each word wrapped in `<span class="w">` (the last word in `<em class="w">`) and a `<br>` after the second word. Style it at `clamp(3.5rem, 12.4vw, 11.6rem)`, line-height .92, `font-variation-settings: "opsz" 96`, and keep words `white-space: nowrap`.
4. Add the splitter script (about 12 lines): for each `.w`, replace its text with one `<span class="ch">` per character, setting `--i` on each and `--n` on the h1; mark the `.w` spans `aria-hidden="true"` so screen readers read the `aria-label` once.
5. Inside `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`, give `.lux .ch` the 112° gradient, `background-size: 260% 100%`, text clipping, `color: transparent`, `animation: sweep 7.5s linear infinite` and the negative staggered delay. Add the rust variant for `.lux em .ch` and the `sweep` keyframes (100% → 0% background-position). Outside the media query, `.lux em .ch { color: var(--rust) }`.
6. Below the h1, a two-column `.hero-row` (1.1fr / .9fr, `align-items: end`) with the lede `<p>` and a flex `.hero-cta` holding the filled CTA and a `<small>` note.
7. Work strip: a section with a flex head (h2 + paragraph, two round `<button>`s with inline SVG chevrons and `data-dir`). The `.strip` is `display: flex`, `overflow-x: auto`, `scroll-snap-type: x mandatory`, hidden scrollbar, `width: calc(100% + var(--gutter))` with matching negative right margin and right padding so it bleeds to the viewport edge. Each `.case` is `position: relative; flex: 0 0 auto; scroll-snap-align: start`, 420 px or 600 px (`.wide`), with a `.plate` div (bordered, linen, `clamp(260px, 34vw, 380px)` tall, `overflow: hidden`) whose `::before`/`::after` draw the pattern from the modifier class (`p-rings`, `p-planks`, `p-grain`, `p-slant`, `p-shelf`, `p-grid`), then an h3 and a `<dl>`.
8. Add the drag script from the Motion section, plus the arrow buttons and keyboard handling.
9. Process: a linen `<section>` with a two-column head and an `<ol class="weeks">` in a four-column grid; solid ink top border, hairline left rules between items via `::before`, italic rust `<time>`, h3 and paragraph. Collapse to 2 then 1 column and drop the rules.
10. Studio: a two-column grid with a `<blockquote>` (Bodoni, `clamp(1.6rem, 3vw, 2.5rem)`, a `<footer>` for the attribution) and a `<dl class="facts">` whose rows are `div { display: grid; grid-template-columns: 1fr 1.6fr }` with hairline bottom borders.
11. Formats: three `<article class="fmt">` rows in a `1.2fr 1fr 1fr auto` grid with solid ink top rules and one bottom rule; Bodoni price with a block-level `<small>`. Reflow to two columns then one.
12. Final CTA: an ink section with a `1.4fr 1fr` grid, h2 at `clamp(2.2rem, 5vw, 4.4rem)` with the italic glow-2 word, paragraph, an outlined paper button and a right-aligned contact line. Then the `<footer>` row.
13. Add `:focus-visible { outline: 2px solid var(--rust); outline-offset: 4px }`, `html { scroll-behavior: smooth }` with a reduced-motion override, and check that the only animations live inside the no-preference media query.
14. Test at 375 px: the h1 wraps to three or four lines without overflow, gutters are 16 px, the strip scrolls by touch with 78vw cards, the weeks and formats stack, and `document.documentElement.scrollWidth` equals the viewport width (keep every absolutely positioned element inside a `position: relative` card so nothing escapes the strip's clip). Confirm one `<h1>` and the header/nav/main/footer landmarks.
