Norrvik harbour, west pier

Seven rooms over the water, and a ketch that leaves at first light.

A small inn in a former chandlery. You sleep above the quay, eat what the boats brought in, and if the wind is kind you are out past the mole before breakfast.

Breakfast and a place on the morning sail are included in every stay.

Three kinds of room, one harbour to look at.

The building was a ship's chandlery until 1991. We kept the beams, the brass, and the draught under the front door.

  • Lamp rooms four rooms, first floor

    Two sash windows onto the pier, a wide bed under the old loading beam, and the quay lamp directly outside. We give you a blind, but nobody has used it.

    €164 a night

  • Keel lofts two rooms, second floor

    Up under the roof, where the sail loft was. A bath at the gable window, a reading chair, and the sound of halyards against masts when the wind backs north.

    €212 a night

  • The quay corner one room, ground floor

    Its own door onto the cobbles, a small stove, and a window at water level. Come in wet from the dinghy and hang everything by the fire.

    €268 a night

The morning on Marit

Marit is a 42-foot wooden ketch built in 1964. She takes six guests and does not hurry. This is roughly how the morning goes.

  1. 06:15

    Coffee on the quayThermos, oilskins if you need them, and a look at the sky with the skipper.

  2. 06:45

    Cast offWe motor out between the moles while the town is still dark.

  3. 07:30

    Sails upMain and mizzen first, then the jib. You can haul, or you can hold your mug and watch.

  4. 08:45

    Breakfast on deckBread from the inn, smoked fish, eggs done on the cabin stove.

  5. 10:30

    Alongside againBack at the west pier with the whole day still ahead of you.

Bring

  • Shoes with soft soles. Boots and heels mark the deck.
  • A warm layer even in July. It is cooler past the mole.
  • Nothing else. Lifejackets, oilskins and breakfast are on board.

If the wind is above 25 knots we stay in and do breakfast by the stove instead. You will know the evening before.
