From the chest to the chart

No wires, no diary, no charging. The patient puts it on and forgets about it, which is the point.

  1. Apply

    Clean the skin, peel, press for ten seconds. The hydrocolloid adhesive holds through showers, sweat and sleep. Patients do it at home from the card in the box.

  2. Wear

    The patch samples at 512 Hz and classifies rhythm on its own processor. Each night the phone app pulls the day's data over Bluetooth. No phone? The patch keeps everything in memory for the cradle.

  3. Read

    Post the patch back in the prepaid sleeve. Within 24 hours of arrival your team has the report: every flagged episode with its full strip, burden by hour, and an export as PDF or FHIR.
